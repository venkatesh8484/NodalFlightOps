AMS Ground Operations Briefing Note

Ground handling at Amsterdam Schiphol (AMS) is coordinated by the Ramp Control
Unit, which reports into the OCC (Operations Control Center). Each aircraft
turnaround is managed by a Turnaround Coordinator who tracks pushback,
de-icing, baggage loading, and catering against the scheduled block time.

Key roles:
- Ramp Agent: handles baggage and cargo loading/unloading on the apron.
- Turnaround Coordinator: owns the overall turnaround timeline for a tail.
- Load Controller: produces the final load sheet and weight-and-balance.
- OCC Duty Manager: escalation point for delays exceeding 15 minutes.

A delay code is assigned to every departure that misses its scheduled
off-block time by more than 3 minutes, using the IATA AHM 730 delay code
table. Recurring delay codes at a station trigger a root-cause review by
the station manager and OCC.
